Incident summary: On 7 January between 14:10 and 16:45 UTC the checkout service returned errors for roughly 30% of requests. The cause was an expired TLS certificate on the internal payments gateway; the renewal job had been failing silently since December. About 1,900 orders failed and customers were not charged. The on-call engineer found the cause after 95 minutes because the alert pointed at the wrong service. We have renewed the certificate, fixed the renewal job, and will add expiry monitoring this week.
