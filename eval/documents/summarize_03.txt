Notice from the Department of Public Works: Alder Avenue will be closed between 5th Street and 9th Street from Monday 2 June to Friday 20 June for water main replacement. Work hours are 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. on weekdays. Traffic will be detoured via Birch Road. Bus route 14 will skip three stops during the closure. Residents will keep driveway access outside work hours. Questions can go to 555-0142.
