Starting 1 March the Reno warehouse moves from two shifts to three. Receiving will run 6 a.m. to 2 p.m., picking and packing 2 p.m. to 10 p.m., and a new overnight shift will handle replenishment and cycle counts. Forklift charging moves to the east dock so that the west dock stays clear for outbound trucks. Supervisors should post the new rosters by 20 February.
