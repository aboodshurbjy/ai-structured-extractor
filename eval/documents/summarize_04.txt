Interim analysis of the phase 2 trial of compound RX-118 in 212 adults with chronic migraine: the drug reduced monthly migraine days by 3.9 compared with 1.6 on placebo, meeting the primary endpoint. However, 11% of patients on the drug had elevated liver enzymes versus 2% on placebo, and four stopped treatment for that reason. The monitoring board recommended continuing the trial with added liver monitoring. The company has delayed its phase 3 decision until the full data are available in the fourth quarter.
